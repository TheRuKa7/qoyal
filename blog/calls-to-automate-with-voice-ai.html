30 phone calls your team can hand to a voice agent

A desk-by-desk list of routine calls that AI voice agents handle well, from sales and support to collections, operations and HR.

If a call follows a script, happens many times a day and ends in an answer someone has to write down, a voice agent can usually make it. Here are thirty, grouped by desk. Each one is a starting point, not a limit.

Sales

  1. New lead qualification. Budget, timeline and need, then a meeting booked.
  2. Missed-call call backs. Ring back within minutes, not the next day.
  3. Site visit and test drive booking. Pick a slot, confirm the address.
  4. Quote follow-ups. Did the quote arrive, any questions, next step.
  5. Event RSVPs. Attending or not, and how many guests.
  6. Win-back calls. Why they stopped buying, and an offer to return.

Support

  1. Where is my order. Read the order back, share status, send tracking.
  2. Cash on delivery confirmation. Confirm before dispatch and cut returns.
  3. Delivery slot booking. Choose a time window that works.
  4. Return pickups. Confirm the address and pickup date.
  5. Refund status. Share where the refund is and when it lands.
  6. Post-service feedback. A rating and one line on what to improve.

Collections and finance

  1. EMI and payment reminders. Before and after the due date.
  2. Promise to pay. Capture the amount, date and mode.
  3. Invoice receipt confirmation. Did it arrive, any disputes.
  4. Renewal reminders. Insurance, subscriptions, AMCs.
  5. KYC follow-ups. Which document is pending and when it will come.

Operations and supply chain

  1. PO acceptance. Confirm the supplier has accepted the order.
  2. Dispatch date follow-ups. Date and quantity, read back.
  3. Delay reasons. What happened and the new date.
  4. Stock and price checks. Availability and rate for a list of items.
  5. Dealer check-ins. Orders, stock levels and issues this week.
  6. Vendor onboarding. Documents, bank details status, first order readiness.

HR and hiring

  1. Candidate screening. Location, experience, expected pay, notice period.
  2. Interview scheduling. Offer slots and send the invite.
  3. Joining confirmation. Date, documents and any blockers.
  4. Employee pulse checks. Short, regular questions for frontline teams.

Healthcare, education and more

  1. Appointment reminders. Confirm, reschedule or cancel.
  2. Report ready alerts. Tell patients or customers their report is ready.
  3. Admission and fee reminders. Enquiry follow-ups, deadlines and dues.
Not on the list? Most teams have a call we have not named. If it is repetitive and ends in an answer, it is a good candidate. Tell us about it.
