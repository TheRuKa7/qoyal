Monitor

Call analysis

Turn any call into a summary, a priority and a list of next steps.

Updated 25 Sep 20261 min read

The analysis page#

Open any call from the call log or a campaign to see everything known about it on one page.

What analysis adds#

Field What it tells you
Executive summary What happened on the call in a few sentences.
Priority Low, medium or high, so your team knows what to look at first.
Issue resolved Whether the purpose of the call was met.
Action items Concrete next steps for your team.
Call context The input values the call started with.
Reported outcome The output values the agent captured.
Technical details Timing and call data for troubleshooting.
Recording and transcript The full conversation, turn by turn.

A call that has not been analysed yet still shows its summary from the agent’s reported outcome.

Running and re-running analysis#

Press Re-analyze on any call with a conversation to run analysis again, for example after you change what you want to know. From the call log or a campaign’s responses, select several calls and re-analyse them together.

Exports#

Export a single call’s full record, download its recording, or copy its call id to share with a colleague.
