Playbooks

Use-case playbooks

Ready-to-copy agent designs for common calls. Each lists the sheet columns, the results to capture, the call flow and what to measure.

Every playbook uses the same building blocks: inputs and outputs, a prompt written in sections, and a campaign per list. Copy one, then adjust the wording and fields to your process.

Supplier dispatch follow-up

Operations · outbound · Hinglish

Confirm when each open purchase order line will ship, and how much.

Inputs (sheet columns)

KeyNotes
supplier_nameshared
po_numberspell digits · primary id
itemshared
open_qtynumber
due_dateshared
supplier_codeinternal

Outputs (results)

KeyType and options
statuschoice: CONFIRMED, PARTIAL, NEW_DATE, CALL_BACK, WRONG_CONTACT
dispatch_datedate after read-back
qty_readynumber
reasonfree text, if late
callback_timefree text

Flow: introduce, confirm the right contact, ask the dispatch date, ask the quantity, read both back, capture a reason if late, close.

Measure: confirmed lines, partial shipments caught, time from call to ERP update.

Design your own

Most teams have a call nobody has named here. Answer five questions and you have an agent:

  1. Who are you calling, and why?This becomes the role and goal section.
  2. What do you already know about them?These are your inputs.
  3. What must you know when you hang up?These are your outputs. Add a status with fixed options.
  4. What usually goes wrong on this call?Write a rule for each: busy, wrong person, dispute, bad line.
  5. Where does the answer go?A download, a report, or your system through the API.

Stuck? Describe the call to us and we will build the first version with you.
