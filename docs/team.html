Manage

Team and access

Decide who can use your workspace, what they can change, and which campaigns they can see.

Workspaces

Your organisation has its own workspace. If you belong to more than one, you choose which to work in after signing in. Sessions expire for safety; when they do, Echo returns you to sign in.

Roles

RoleCan doSees
Org adminEverything, plus inviting people, changing roles and managing groups.All campaigns in the workspace.
Campaign managerCreate and run campaigns.Campaigns they created, plus those of any group they belong to.
UserWork with campaigns shared with them.Only their group’s campaigns.

The Team page is visible to org admins only, and every permission is enforced on the server, not only hidden in the menu.

Agent access

Seeing and editing prompts is a separate permission, agent access, granted per person and independent of role. It keeps prompts in the hands of the people who own them. Ask your account team to grant or remove it.

Groups

Groups control who sees which campaigns. Create a group for each team or region, add members, rename or delete it as your organisation changes.

Managing users

  • Invite a person with their email, a temporary password and a role.
  • Change a role: pick the new role and press Update. Changes are never applied on a stray click.
  • Remove a person to end their access.

System status

Settings shows whether Echo and the services it depends on are up. Check it first if calls stop going out.
