Monitor

Dashboard and call log

See how the whole operation is doing, then drill into any call.

The funnel

Overview opens with a funnel that reads top to bottom. Each tile carries a note that says what it counted, so a percentage is never shown without its base.

TileWhat it counts
PlannedRows uploaded across your campaigns.
Will dialPlanned rows that can be called. Rows whose agent has no calling line are counted as blocked.
AttemptedRows dialled at least once.
Connected rateAnswered calls out of attempted.
Goal achievedCalls where the agent confirmed what it was for, out of connected.
Inconclusive rateConnected calls that settled nothing either way.
Callbacks openCallers who asked to be rung back.
Tech failuresCalls that were answered, then had no audio either way.
Avg durationAverage call length, with turns per call.
UsageAI usage for the period.

The call log

Below the funnel is every call, newest first: time, contact, number, agent, length, status, campaign, turns and a one-line summary you can expand in place.

StatusMeaning
On airThe call is live right now.
CompletedThe call ended with a conversation.
No audioThe call was answered but nothing was said either way. This is the failure to look for.

Filters and downloads

  • Search by name or number.
  • Time range: last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time.
  • Length: under 30 seconds, 30 seconds to 2 minutes, over 2 minutes.

Download gives you the call log as a spreadsheet with exactly the filters on screen, so the file and the table always agree.

Open a call

Click a row to open the call beside the list: its transcript, the values it captured, the inputs it started with, and the recording. Download the transcript as text, or open full call analysis.

Bulk actions

Tick several calls to export them as CSV, download their audio, or re-run analysis on all of them at once.

Live calls

When calls are in progress, the header shows how many are on the line, and they appear in the log as On air while they run.
