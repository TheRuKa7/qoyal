3. Your calls, your responsibility#

You decide who your agents call, what they say and why. You are the party responsible for each call made or received through the Service, and you must:

  • Have permission to call. Hold a lawful basis or the consent required in each country you call, and keep records of it for at least five years or longer if the law requires.
  • Follow calling laws where the person is. For example, in India the TRAI Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, Do Not Disturb preferences, registered headers and number series, and permitted calling hours; in the United States the TCPA, the Telemarketing Sales Rule and Do Not Call lists; in the UK and EU, PECR and GDPR; and equivalent rules in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Singapore and elsewhere.
  • Be honest on the call. Make sure the agent identifies your business and the purpose of the call, does not claim to be a human when asked, and discloses that it is automated wherever the law requires.
  • Handle recordings lawfully. Tell people when calls are recorded, and get consent where the law requires consent from everyone on the call.
  • Honour opt-outs. Stop calling people who ask you to stop, and keep your suppression lists up to date.
  • Use accurate caller ID. Never spoof or hide the number you call from.
  • Follow sector rules. For example, the RBI Fair Practices Code for collections, and health, insurance or financial rules that apply to your calls.

We may set calling-hour limits, pause campaigns or block numbers when we reasonably believe calls break the law or these terms.
