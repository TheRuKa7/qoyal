Acceptable Use Policy

What you may and may not do with Echo voice agents, numbers and APIs. Part of the Echo Terms of Service.

Last updated 30 Sep 2026

This policy is part of the Terms of Service. It applies to everyone who uses Echo through your account, including your team and anyone using your API keys. If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, ask us at ai@moglix.com before you launch.

Calls must be lawful and expected#

Do not use Echo to:

  • call people without the consent or lawful basis required where they are, or call numbers on a Do Not Call or Do Not Disturb list when the law does not allow it;
  • call outside permitted calling hours in the person's time zone;
  • keep calling someone who has asked you to stop;
  • hide or spoof your caller ID, or call from unregistered headers or number series where registration is required;
  • make political, election or campaign calls unless you meet every rule that applies to them;
  • place calls to emergency numbers, or tie up lines with high-volume or repeated calls.

Be honest with the people you call#

Do not use Echo to:

  • pretend to be a real person, government body, bank, company or brand you are not authorised to represent;
  • tell a person the agent is human when they ask, or hide that the call is automated where the law requires disclosure;
  • run scams, phishing, fake prizes, fake debts or any deceptive offer;
  • ask for OTPs, passwords, full card numbers, CVVs or bank logins over the phone.

Treat people with respect#

Do not use Echo to:

  • harass, threaten, shame or intimidate anyone, including in debt collection;
  • disclose someone's debt or personal details to family, colleagues or other third parties;
  • target children, or design agents that sound like children;
  • promote violence, hate, self-harm or sexual content;
  • discriminate unlawfully in lending, housing, hiring, insurance or similar decisions.

Voices and content#

  • Clone or imitate a real person's voice only with their documented consent.
  • Do not create deepfakes or content that infringes someone's copyright, trademark, privacy or publicity rights.
  • Do not use Echo to give medical, legal or financial advice that should come from a qualified professional, or as the only means of handling emergencies.

Data#

  • Upload only the data you need for the call.
  • Do not upload special category or sensitive data (health, biometric, card or government id data) unless your order form covers it and you have the required consent.
  • Keep consent and opt-out records for at least five years or longer where required.

The Service itself#

Do not:

  • probe, scan or test the Service's security without our written permission;
  • get around usage limits, access controls or billing;
  • overload the Service or use it to attack others;
  • reverse engineer, scrape or copy the Service, or use its output to train a competing voice or language model;
  • resell or share access without an agreement with us.

What happens if this policy is broken#

We may warn you, pause a campaign, block numbers, disable an agent, suspend the account, or end the agreement, depending on the seriousness of the issue and the risk to others. Where we can, we will tell you first and give you a chance to fix it. We may report illegal activity to the authorities and cooperate with lawful requests.

To report misuse of Echo, including a call you received, email ai@moglix.com with the number, date and time.
