How the estimate is worked out#

  • Order value followed up on time = calls a month × share Echo handles × share that reach an outcome × share of orders that slip × average order value. It counts the value of at-risk orders that get a completed follow-up. It does not assume every one of them is saved.
  • Calling hours taken off your team = calls a month × share Echo handles × (call length + dialling and notes) ÷ 60.
  • People's worth of time = those hours ÷ (6.5 productive hours × 24 working days).
  • Team time value a year = people's worth of time × monthly cost of one calling agent × 12. This is time your team can move to other work. It is not a claim that anyone's job changes.
  • Inputs are limited to practical ranges, and the monthly order book being called (calls × order value) is capped at 500 crore rupees, so the calculator cannot show results that no real operation would see.
  • Currencies use fixed rates for illustration only (September 2026): 1 USD = ₹88, 1 AED = ₹24, 1 SAR = ₹23.5, 1 SGD = ₹68, 1 EUR = ₹103, 1 GBP = ₹118.

A pilot replaces these assumptions with your own baseline. Use of this calculator is covered by the Terms of Service.
