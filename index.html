01:48 Confirming

“Haan” is not a yes.

On a shop floor, haan means “go on”. So Qoyal never books on a filler. It reads the promise back, number by number, and waits for a real yes. Only then does the stamp come down.

02:20 Behind the call

Rows in. Calls out. Outcomes back.

  1. 01 · Connect

    Bring the list

    Open orders, tickets or leads arrive by API, CSV, Google Sheet or CRM webhook. Qoyal cleans them and groups them: one call per person, not one per row.

  2. 02 · Call

    Dial inside the window

    Calls start only inside the hours you set, over Ozonetel, Exotel or Twilio numbers. No answer, busy, or “call me at 4”: it tries again.

  3. 03 · Write back

    Post the outcome

    Every call ends in a structured outcome with the transcript and recording attached, posted to your system by webhook the same day.
