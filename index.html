FAQ

FAQ: what enterprises ask first.

Straight answers on cost, calling rules, data and getting started.

DocsNo-code guideBuild, test and run agents from a sheet. ROIROI calculatorWhat one workflow is worth with your own numbers.
What is echo by Vanik?

echo by Vanik is enterprise voice AI. It runs the supplier and customer calls behind your orders, deliveries and cash, confirms every value on the call, and writes the outcome into your ERP or helpdesk the same day.

How is echo different from other voice AI platforms?

Most voice AI ends at a transcript. Echo starts from your system of record, cleans and groups the open records into calls, confirms every commitment by reading it back, and writes a fixed outcome back at line level. Compliance checks run before any number is dialled.

Which use case should we start with?

Start where money waits on a phone call: PO acceptance, delivery dates, quote reminders or order status. Pick one plant or one book, measure the baseline for two weeks, then run echo against it.

How does echo contribute to revenue?

Each workflow is tied to a business event: POs accepted sooner, delivery dates confirmed, slipping orders rescued, more quotes returned, invoices and payment questions resolved on the first call. Echo reports on those events and on cost per resolved record.

Which workflows does echo run today?

Supplier operations: post-PO acceptance, delivery-date confirmation, lead-time follow-up, material-ready checks, quote reminders and plant KYC. Customer support: order status, invoice and payment queries, and ticket intake. A new workflow is a filter and a prompt on the same engine.

Which systems does echo connect to?

Your ERP, helpdesk and sourcing tools through the API (in developer preview), signed webhooks and exports, or scheduled sheets for a fast start. How outcomes reach your ERP is set up with your team during the pilot. Outcomes are written at the grain your system uses, down to the PO line.

Which languages does echo speak?

22+ languages, switching mid-call when the caller does: English, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Indonesian, Malay, Japanese, Mandarin, and Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese and Urdu. Callers can switch language mid sentence, and each agent is tuned to the accents of your market.

Where is our data kept?

In the region you choose. Call audio, transcripts and lists are hosted in-region, in the region you choose. Recordings are kept 90 days and the audit trail 5 years, or to your local rules. Access is isolated per tenant and controlled by role. A security pack is available under NDA.

How do we start?

With a paid pilot on one plant or one team, on one workflow, measured against your own baseline. We set up the agent with you and review the first calls together before you scale.

How is echo priced?

You pay for the minutes echo talks; unanswered calls are free. Most teams start with a paid trial on one workflow, measured against their own baseline, and the rate falls as volume grows. Talk to an expert for a quote at your volume.

Is AI calling legal?

Yes, when it follows the local rules. echo checks do-not-call registries, keeps to calling hours, records consent and says it is an AI assistant where the law asks for it. Each country gets its own rule pack before the first call.

Will people know they are talking to AI?

echo says so at the start of the call where rules require it, and many teams choose to say it everywhere. What people notice most is that the call is short, in their language, and reads every answer back before it is saved.

Can echo replace our calling team?

It takes the routine calls: confirmations, reminders and follow-ups. Your team keeps the exceptions echo flags, such as disputes and changed orders, so the same people handle more accounts.

Do you work outside India?

Yes. Echo is set up country by country. Each country gets a rule pack for local do-not-call registries, calling hours, consent and AI disclosure, plus the languages and hosting region your team needs. We scope it at the start, and the timeline can be expedited to match your country and go-live date.
