What is Cognilix Echo?

Cognilix Echo is enterprise voice AI. It runs the supplier and customer calls behind your orders, deliveries and cash, confirms every value on the call, and writes the outcome into your ERP or helpdesk the same day.

How is Echo different from other voice AI platforms?

Most voice AI ends at a transcript. Echo starts from your system of record, cleans and groups the open records into calls, confirms every commitment by reading it back, and writes a fixed outcome back at line level. Compliance checks run before any number is dialled.

How does Echo work with other Cognilix products?

Echo is the voice layer of the Cognilix suite. It confirms POs and quotes for Cerebra, onboards suppliers for Cortex, brings bidders to Connexa auctions, gets firm dates for Cogniplan and answers buyers for Cognimart. Each outcome is written back into the product that owns the record.

Which use case should we start with?

Start where money waits on a phone call: supplier order confirmation, quote chasing, receivables or collections. Pick one plant or one book, measure the baseline for two weeks, then run Echo against it.

How does Echo contribute to revenue?

Each workflow is tied to a business event: POs accepted sooner, delivery dates confirmed, slipping orders rescued, more quotes returned, invoices and payment questions resolved on the first call. Echo reports on those events and on cost per resolved record.

Which workflows does Echo run today?

Supplier operations: post-PO acceptance, delivery-date confirmation, lead-time follow-up, material-ready checks, quote reminders and plant KYC. Customer support: order status, invoice and payment queries, and ticket intake. A new workflow is a filter and a prompt on the same engine.

More questions
Which systems does Echo connect to?

Your ERP, helpdesk and sourcing tools through writeback connectors, signed webhooks and the API, or scheduled sheets for a fast start. Outcomes are written at the grain your system uses, down to the PO line.

Which languages does Echo speak?

Hindi, English and Hinglish today, including callers who switch between them mid sentence. More Indian languages are being added.

Where is our data kept?

Call audio, transcripts and lists stay in India. Recordings are kept 90 days and the audit trail 5 years. Access is isolated per tenant and controlled by role. A security pack is available under NDA.

How do we start?

With a paid pilot on one plant or one team, on one workflow, measured against your own baseline. We set up the agent with you and review the first calls together before you scale.

How is Echo priced?

Pricing depends on your volumes and the workflows you run. Talk to sales and we will share a quote for your case.

Do you work with companies outside India?

Yes. India runs in production today. Rule packs for the UAE, Singapore, the United States and the EU and UK roll out through 2027, starting where Cognilix already operates. Each pack covers local do-not-call registries, calling hours, consent and AI disclosure.
